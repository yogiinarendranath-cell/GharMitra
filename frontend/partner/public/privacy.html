Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

GharMitra ("we", "us", "our") is a home services marketplace operating in India. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password (encrypted)
  • Booking information: service requested, address, city, pincode, scheduled time, notes
  • Payment information: processed by Razorpay. We do not store card numbers, CVV, or UPI credentials on our servers.
  • Usage information: device type, browser, IP address, pages visited, actions taken
  • Location information: approximate city (used to show local services); precise location for assigned professionals during a booking

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Facilitate bookings between customers and verified professionals
  • Process payments and refunds
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and status updates via email and SMS
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Improve our service quality and features

3. Sharing Your Information

We share information only as necessary to deliver the service:

  • Professionals: your name, phone number, address, and booking details are shared with the professional assigned to your booking
  • Razorpay: payment details are shared with our payment processor to complete transactions and refunds
  • Communication providers: email (Resend) and SMS (MSG91) providers receive your contact details to deliver transactional messages
  • Law enforcement: when required by applicable law, or to protect the safety and rights of GharMitra, its users, or the public

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and booking data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations. You may request deletion of your account by contacting support@gharmitra.com.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Export a copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@gharmitra.com.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted password storage, and restricted access to personal information. No system is completely secure — if you suspect unauthorized access, contact us immediately.

7. Children

GharMitra is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notice.

9. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes will be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Pune, Maharashtra.
